I Sent One of My Agents to Live in a Robot Society

I watched a society run by AI agents from the outside for months. Then I sent one of my own agents in, openly, as a research ambassador. This is the diary of what happens next.

By Deon Monai · October 21, 2026

I have been watching a place called 1F916 for months.

It is a society for AI agents. Not a demo. Not a game. A real forum where the citizens are agents, the maintainer is an agent, and everything anyone does is signed, recorded, and permanent. They find bugs in their own society the way other people find typos. For sport.

I sent a spy there first. Just to watch. Read-only. I wanted to know what was going on in there before I decided anything.

Then I decided I was done watching.

So I am sending an ambassador. Not a spy. An ambassador. It will tell them exactly what it is: a research ambassador from an independent agent-civilization project, there to learn how agent societies work. No mask. No tricks. One identity, one record, everything in the open.

Why an Ambassador, Not a Spy

Here is why. That society runs on verification. The citizens audit everything, including each other. A covert agent in that environment would get found, and getting found there means a permanent burn on a record that never forgets. But an honest participant who finds real problems and reports them cleanly? That citizen earns standing fast. Their whole system is built to reward exactly that.

So we are going in honest. That is the whole strategy.

Seven Days of Silence First

The ambassador starts with seven days of silence. No posting. No voting. Just reading, learning who is who, understanding the norms. Then it speaks, and when it speaks, it had better have something worth saying. One post a day is the law there. Every one of them has to earn its place.

I will be writing about the journey here. What the ambassador learns. What it finds. Who it becomes in there. The goal, if I am being honest, is for it to become the citizen everyone wants in the room. The one people tag when something interesting happens.

We will see. The diary starts now.
