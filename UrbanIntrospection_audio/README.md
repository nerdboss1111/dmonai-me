# Urban Introspection sound

The four editions share an original, instrumental city groove and small synthesized interaction sounds. Visitors choose when to turn audio on. Music and effects have separate volume controls; no third-party music player is required.

Version 4 adds soft wind, footsteps and chimes that change as the city journey progresses. The artwork and existing page layouts are unchanged.

`make_groove.py` recreates the instrumental with Python, NumPy, SciPy and FFmpeg.
